FAQ

Answers to common questions

What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization is the practice of improving how often and how favorably AI engines mention your brand. It complements SEO, which targets classic search results.

Which markets and languages do you cover?

We start with Chile in Spanish and expand across LATAM. Prompts are written in your customers' language and market.

Why show percentages instead of one rank?

AI answers change from run to run. We repeat each prompt and report how often you appear, with a confidence range, so the trend is reliable.

Are API answers the same as what consumers see?

Not exactly. Consumer apps add personalization and different system prompts. Treat the numbers as a consistent benchmark over time rather than exact consumer exposure.

Who is it for?

Marketing and e-commerce teams at brands and retailers, where the CMO or marketing lead decides on spend.

How do I get access?

We're onboarding a small group of pilot brands. Join the waitlist and we'll reach out. Early adopters get preferential terms.
